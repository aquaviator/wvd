## MTD-UK-001

**Exact claim:** Making Tax Digital for Income Tax became mandatory from 6 April 2026 for qualifying sole traders and landlords whose qualifying income for 2024/25 was more than £50,000.  
**Source:** HMRC, [Before you use this guide](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/use-making-tax-digital-for-income-tax/before-you-use-this-guide)  
**Verified / review by / expiry:** 27 Aug 2026 / 27 Oct 2026 / none  
**May be used:** Product context, FAQ, tutorial; no future-tense urgency.  
**Risk/notes:** Historical commencement must not imply that every landlord is mandated.
